# Read-only reassessment of the PPK2 ON blocker

2026-09-25. No device access, wiring change or ON authorization is made here.

Nordic explicitly supports externally USB-powered DUTs in Ampere Meter mode; it recommends a laboratory supply for better accuracy, not as a universal prerequisite. Therefore **absence of a DMM/lab supply alone is not a documented prohibition of this manufacturer-described use**. A hypothetical USB tolerance excursion is not evidence that this particular VIN is overvoltage. [Nordic Ampere Meter guide](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ug_ppk2/page/ug/ppk/measure_current_ampere_meter.html).

This does not erase the specified limits: Nordic lists external/DUT voltage 0.8–5.0 V and Ampere Meter continuous current up to 1 A. The wider 4.5–5.5 V specification applies to PPK2's own Micro-USB supply, not automatically its measurement VIN. We should state unmeasured VIN as a measurement/qualification limitation, neither invent a software VIN reading nor declare a proven electrical violation. [Nordic power specifications](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ug_ppk2/page/ug/ppk/ppk_power_supply_specs.html), [Nordic admissible current](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ug_ppk2/page/ug/ppk/ppk_max_dut_current.html).

The concrete contrary evidence is the user's **previous** steady-red LD3 after earlier pulses. UM3300 identifies LD3 as STLINK-V3EC power status; TN1235 states steady red corresponds to detected overcurrent with automatic target-supply shutoff. That observation supports an earlier protection event, not a diagnosis of short circuit, permanent damage, a currently asserted fault, or a proven hot-load/inrush cause. Replugging means its persistence now cannot be assumed. [ST UM3300](https://www.st.com.cn/resource/en/user_manual/um3300-discovery-kit-with-stm32n657x0-mcu-stmicroelectronics.pdf), [ST TN1235 section 7](https://www.st.com/resource/en/technical_note/dm00290229-overview-of-st-link-derivatives-stmicroelectronics.pdf).

The selected CN6 Type-A-to-C source has a documented approximately 550 mA limitation close to the board's consumption. This is a concrete startup/load-budget concern independent of PPK2's 1 A measurement rating. Adding a cable to CN18 does not by itself prove the measured JP2[1–2] path now obtains the CN18 supply while JP2[3–4] remains open. No new source selection or higher-budget route was verified here. [ST UM3300 sections 6.1 and 7.4](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/um3300-discovery-kit-with-stm32n657x0-mcu-stmicroelectronics.pdf).

Current saved evidence establishes only that the passive tool sent metadata/START/STOP and did not change the DUT switch. The last deliberately commanded state was OFF before the replug; there is no live output-state query proving what re-enumeration retained. The roughly zero passive conditional-current mean and low ST-LINK target-reference reading do not prove a new fault or qualify measurement VIN. They also do not establish powered N6/NPU execution.

Thus the defensible remaining issue is unresolved startup/protection behavior and the actual selected power path/current budget, **not** a blanket rule that USB requires a voltmeter. A fresh contemporaneous indicator/state observation and an explicitly reviewed startup/source plan can inform the owner's next bounded action without asserting an exact electrical threshold. Repeating the unchanged earlier ON attempt solely because raw capture is now fixed would not explain the earlier protection event. No new ON, retry, source-mode conversion or supply/wiring operation is proposed as already safe or authorized by this review.
