# SM07M (STM32N6570-DK) energy measurement — erratum (2026-09-30 08:42 +08)

This was found during the ESP32-S3 measurement: `results/power_esp32s3_20260930/PROTOCOL.md`, Amendment 2 and Erratum 1. The registered `PROTOCOL.md` of this folder is left unchanged.

1. **The sham attribution is not established.**
   - SM07M build_03 (`tools/n6_deployment/firmware_sram_measure/build_03/n6_sram.elf`) inlines its timer busy-wait into 7 copies in `main()`: 0x3406514e, 0x3406516c, 0x3406522c, 0x34065248, 0x3406527a, 0x3406532e, 0x34065422.
   - The first two are 30 bytes apart, consistent with the sham's HIGH and LOW halves. The mapping is inferred from code order and spacing; it was not traced instruction by instruction.
   - On the ESP32-S3, the same structure (different wait copies for the sham's two halves) produced a code-placement sham offset of 0.66 mA.
   - The N6 sham ΔI (−0.03…−0.09 mA) may therefore include code placement, and its attribution to the PPK2 D0 input network is not established.
   - The RA4E1 protocol's use of this N6 sham as supporting evidence is withdrawn: RA4E1 RESULTS, Erratum revision.
2. **Unaffected:** the gross headline of 49.41 mJ per inference, because BENCH windows contain no wait, and the eligibility of the sessions.
3. **Inherits the ambiguity:** the incremental (≈ 0), because its IDLE spin is one inlined wait copy, and any marker-state band derived from the sham.
4. **Type-B wording:**
   - PPK2 codes 0–4 are the user guide's ranges R1–R5.
   - The ±20 % gain term is a typical-spec envelope, not a guarantee. It is the UG v1.0.1 Table 9 accuracy plus offset rows, both typical, for averaged readout.
   - The registered VIN band (4.4–5.25 V) is unchanged.
